Custom Software vs Off-the-Shelf Solutions: Making the Right Choice

We’ll be honest: custom software development is not necessary for every business. The right choice depends on your workflow complexity, the required functionality, implementation costs and your long-term needs.

What Makes Custom Software Different?

Off-the-shelf tools usually expect you to adjust your process to fit them. Custom applications are designed around your specific business processes instead. That gives you greater flexibility over workflows, reporting and integration requirements.

When Is Ready-Made Software a Practical Choice?

If a ready-made tool does the job, it’s often the sensible pick. Ready-made software works well when its standard functionality already meets your business needs and faster implementation matters more to you than extensive customization.

The initial purchase price is only part of the picture. Before you decide, compare licensing fees, development expenses, integration effort, upgrades and ongoing support. A tool that is cheaper to buy can end up costing more to run.
