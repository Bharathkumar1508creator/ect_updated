Is Your Business IT Infrastructure Ready for Everyday Demands?

An IT infrastructure health check helps identify weak points before planning upgrades. Instead of replacing systems unnecessarily, begin by examining what works and what needs attention.

Step 01

Evaluate Network and Internet Reliability

  • Check frequent connection drops and Wi-Fi coverage.
  • Review internet capacity against employee needs.
  • Identify devices experiencing recurring problems.
Step 02

Review Business Email and Cloud Workspace Configuration

  • Verify mailbox access and administrator controls.
  • Review DNS and email authentication settings.
  • Check account permissions and inactive users.
Step 03

Assess Server Performance and Storage Requirements

  • Review resource usage and available storage.
  • Identify unsupported or outdated software.
  • Check application availability and system logs.
Step 04

Identify Backup, Access and Security Gaps

  • Verify that essential backups are available.
  • Review access permissions and account security.
  • Confirm recovery procedures are documented.
Step 05

Prioritize Infrastructure Improvements

Classify issues according to operational impact, urgency, security considerations, and available budget. An assessment can guide future IT infrastructure implementation decisions.
