Hiring Process

Simple, Transparent,
and Professional

Our hiring process is simple, transparent, and designed to identify top talent while ensuring a smooth candidate experience.

Apply & Get Shortlisted

Apply through our careers page or job portals. Our recruitment team reviews every application and shortlists candidates whose skills and experience match the role.

Screening & Assessment

Selected candidates complete an initial screening and practical assessment to evaluate technical knowledge, problem-solving, and role suitability.

Technical & Panel Interview

Meet with our technical experts and panel members to discuss your expertise, communication skills, and fit for the position.

HR Discussion

Discuss the role, company culture, career opportunities, compensation, and expectations to ensure a great fit for both you and ECT9.

Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive an offer letter and begin a structured onboarding process designed for a smooth and successful start.
